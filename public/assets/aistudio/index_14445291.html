Camera Guidance for Best Accuracy

Follow these tips when testing on laptops or phones

1
Position Your Hand (30–50 cm): Keep your palm and all 5 fingers clearly inside the frame without overlapping.
2
Hold Steady for 1 Second: EjjiLink AI requires 5 stable high-confidence frames (>80%) before locking and speaking.
3
Good Front Lighting: Avoid strong backlighting from windows behind you so MediaPipe can track all 21 hand landmarks.

Why We Built EjjiLink AI

“A story about bringing people closer—using accessible AI to turn silent gestures into shared understanding.”

Inclusion Through Technology

Bridging Communication Barriers in Ethiopia

In Ethiopia, rich languages and signing systems exist side by side, yet communication barriers can still isolate members of the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community. Our goal at The Ethio-Tech Initiative was to build a tool that feels familiar, respectful, and works instantly—without expensive hardware.

Built by student innovators at Holy Trinity Cathedral General Secondary School in Addis Ababa, EjjiLink AI focuses on stability and trust. Instead of overloading the system with dozens of unreliable signs, we engineered 6 essential everyday gestures with high-confidence locking.
